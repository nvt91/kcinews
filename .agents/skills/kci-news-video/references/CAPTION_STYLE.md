# KCI social captions — natural Vietnamese, short and relevant

Owner editorial update — 26 October 2026. Applies to all NEW Korea Capital Insight news videos (KR_AM, KR_MID, KR_CLOSE, MACRO21 and GLOBAL_US) and captions recorded in the existing KCI News Google Sheet. This is a copywriting policy, not approval to publish.

## Audience and voice
Write as a knowledgeable, approachable Vietnamese market observer speaking to viewers who follow Korean equities, the Korean economy and global drivers. Use straightforward Vietnamese, a little warmth and specificity, **not a press-release or generic AI-marketing tone**. Every caption must stand on its own and make sense without reading the full video transcript.

## Standard caption
- **Aim for 30–65 Vietnamese words before hashtags** (usually 2–3 short sentences). Allow shorter when one precise statement is sufficient. Do not pad merely to hit a word target.
- Open naturally with the actual news angle or a simple, truthful hook. Name the relevant company/index/event when it helps the reader.
- Include only one clear takeaway or the question viewers genuinely care about. A simple balanced question or gentle “theo dõi KCI” is fine **if appropriate**, never a stacked engagement-bait CTA.
- Be **mộc mạc, thân thiện, dễ hiểu**. Favor “Điều gì đang kéo thị trường lên?” over “Bức tranh vĩ mô đã mở ra dư địa tăng trưởng đột phá”. Avoid hype (“chấn động”, “nóng bỏng”, “cổ phiếu sẽ bùng nổ”), template intros, corporate clichés, FOMO, fake certainty and lengthy disclaimers.
- No Markdown bold or asterisks in text intended to paste into Facebook/Instagram. Use 0–1 suitable emoji, only if natural.
- Respect dates/venues: no old closing price presented as current, no forecast treated as fact. Source links can live in the accompanying source ledger when they would clutter the social caption; do not conceal a necessary qualifier or imply personal investment advice.

## Hashtags — 3–5, relevant and topical
Use **3–5 hashtags** at the end of the caption, never stuffed into sentences. Combine:
1. One stable brand/topic identifier, normally `#KoreaCapitalInsight`.
2. One or two directly relevant audience/search tags, e.g. `#ChungKhoanHanQuoc`, `#KinhTeHanQuoc`, `#KOSPI`, `#KOSDAQ`.
3. One or two genuinely topical discovery tags grounded in that edition, e.g. `#SKhynix`, `#SamsungElectronics`, `#Fed`, `#LaiSuat`, `#BanDan` — only when the video actually covers them.

Prioritize hashtags currently used by the relevant audience when this can be checked near publication through real platform/search evidence. **Never assert that a hashtag is trending without verification** or append unrelated viral hashtags merely for reach (e.g. entertainment memes on a finance video). If live platform trend data are inaccessible, use well-established and specifically relevant tags without calling them proven trends. No promise of virality.

## Optional structure
Opening factual angle (one sentence). One meaningful implication or balanced question (one sentence). Then the hashtag line. Do **not** reproduce the video’s full 90–110-second voice script in the caption.

## Final caption QC
- <=65 words is a **target**, not a license to drop crucial timing/qualifiers.
- 3–5 topic-matching hashtags; if none can be verified as trending, avoid saying they are.
- Natural to read aloud and directly paste to Facebook or Instagram; no bold formatting.
- No invented event, quote, return, audience benefit or popularity claim.
- Each day's caption should reflect the unique news and not repeat the same opening/CTA pattern.
- Preserve manual owner edits to existing KCI News Sheet captions (column D). Only populate blank cells or explicitly requested revisions.
